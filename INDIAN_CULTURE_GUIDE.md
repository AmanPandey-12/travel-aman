## Indian National Colors

### Flag Colors Symbolism
The Indian tricolor represents:

```
🟠 Saffron (#FF9933) - Courage & Sacrifice
⚪ White (#FFFFFF)   - Truth & Purity  
🟢 Green (#138808)   - Growth & Prosperity
```

### Implementation in TravelAman
- **Primary Color:** Saffron (#FF9933) - Used for primary buttons, accents
- **Secondary Color:** Green (#138808) - Used for body text, secondary elements
- **Background:** White (#FFFFFF) - Main background color
